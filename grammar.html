Grammar · Lesson 1

Adjective agreement — les accords

Learn the rule, then type the missing adjective. Spelling and accents count, and your exercise progress is saved.

1. Match the noun

In French, most adjectives agree with the noun they describe in gender and number. The usual pattern is masculine singular → add -e for feminine; add -s for plural. Some adjectives are irregular.

Masculine

un garçon petit
des garçons petits

Feminine

une fille petite
des filles petites

2. Watch these important exceptions

sportif → sportive · heureux → heureuse · beau → belle · blanc → blanche · nouveau → nouvelle

Adjectives already ending in -e usually keep that ending in the feminine: un élève calme / une élève calme. Colour adjectives can have exceptions: des robes orange, but des robes roses.
